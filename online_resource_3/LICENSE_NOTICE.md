# Software and research record permissions

The authors authorized public release and selected MIT for their own research software on 10 November 2026. `LICENSE` contains that grant. The grant covers author-owned executable code and accompanying software documentation in this package. Third-party code retains its own copyright and licence notices.

The adapted missingness-avoiding tree is preserved under its upstream MIT licence at `training/external_baseline_20261109/vendor/malearn/LICENSE.md`. Its source commit and extraction records are retained. Original notices remain attached to the corresponding vendor files.

The MIT software licence does not relicense the external datasets. Their source identities, attribution and notices are listed in `DATA_ACCESS.md`. The APS training CSV is acquired from UCI with its preserved fingerprint and Scania GPL v3-or-later notice; license text is supplied in licenses/GPL-3.0.txt. Other raw features are also acquired separately with fingerprint checks. APS's current repository page and historical Scania notice are both retained in the data notice. Numerical research records retain their research context and original data-source notices.

Frozen sources preserve original wording, old absolute path labels and old scientific terminology as provenance records. Current annotations and manuscript definitions govern interpretation. Public access and author authorization establish a distribution decision. Hashes and arithmetic agreement establish computational consistency within their stated scope.
